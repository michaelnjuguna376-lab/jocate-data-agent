# Market brief: the Potential Partners agent's knowledge base

What Jocate knows about its market. The agent reads this before every run, and treats it as a starting point to verify and extend rather than as ground truth. The figures come from the published Insights articles at https://jocateorg.com/insights/ (each cites its sources).

## What Jocate sells
- Seasoned industrial firewood (eucalyptus and other plantation wood), cut and sized for the firebox.
- Agro-residues: macadamia shells, coffee husks, sawdust and off-cuts, and blends for co-firing.
- Scheduled delivery sized to each boiler's consumption, with buffer stock for peaks and rainy seasons.
- Track record: more than 10 years supplying EABL, Bidco Africa, Prime Cartons, Sunflag Textiles, Kimfay, Edible Oil Refineries and Golden Africa.

## Why demand is rising (2026)
- Diesel hit a record **KSh 242.92/L** in May 2026 and stood at KSh 227.76/L in November (EPRA, Nairobi).
- At May prices, useful heat from diesel cost about **KSh 7,900/GJ**, against about **KSh 1,200–2,200/GJ** for seasoned firewood including the extra freight.
- EPRA's fuel energy cost charge added KSh 3.06–3.51/kWh to power bills during 2026, which raises the cost of electric heating too.
- Q1 2026 fuel-oil consumption fell 10.6% while diesel rose 9.9%: industry is moving off furnace oil.
- Unga Farm Care's diesel-to-biomass switch (Feb 2026) is projected to cut steam costs by about 45%.
- Kenya's firewood demand (about 18.7M m³) exceeds sustainable supply (about 13.65M m³). Reliable, plantation-backed supply is the selling point.

## Who needs steam: sector heat profiles (Jocate expertise)
**Tier A: continuous, high steam demand (best fit)**
| Sector | Typical heat use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tea factories | Withering, drying | About 1M t of firewood a year nationally; many KTDA and estate factories; co-firing at 75:25 |
| Breweries, distilleries, ethanol | Brewing, distilling, CIP cleaning | Large, steady loads; ESG targets common |
| Edible-oil refining, soap, detergents | Refining, deodorising, saponification | Existing Jocate clients in this sector prove the fit |
| Dairy processing | Pasteurising, UHT, powder, CIP | Central and Rift dairy belts sit close to supply |
| Sugar mills | Process steam | Often self-sufficient on bagasse, but buy fuel in the off-season or during shortages |
| Textiles | Dyeing, finishing, washing | Eldoret, Thika, Athi River EPZ |
| Paper, tissue, packaging | Drying, corrugation | Corrugators run steam all day |
| Resins, adhesives, chemicals | Reactor heating, kerosene-linked processes | KAM flagged resin makers as hit by the 2026 kerosene rise |
| Animal feed and milling | Pellet conditioning | Unga's 2026 switch is the template |
| Food and beverage processing | Cooking, canning, juice pasteurising | — |

**Tier B: moderate or intermittent demand**
Hospitals and large hotels (laundry and kitchens), boarding schools and universities (cooking boilers), commercial laundries, tobacco curing, flower-farm heating, large bakeries.

## Signals that a company is ready to switch (score these)
1. A documented fossil steam fuel: furnace oil (HFO), industrial diesel oil (IDO), diesel or LPG boilers.
2. Public energy-efficiency, net-zero or ESG commitments, or a recent energy audit.
3. Expansion: a new plant, extra lines or higher output (more steam needed).
4. A published tender or supplier-registration call for firewood, briquettes or biomass. Watch KTDA factories, public institutions and county governments.
5. Peers in the same sector or town have already switched (social proof).
6. Location within the short-haul radius of a Jocate supply belt.

## Where: demand hotspots and supply belts
See `hotspots.json` for coordinates, sectors and distances. In summary:
- **Nairobi Industrial Area, Baba Dogo, Ruaraka, Athi River, Mlolongo**: the main demand hub. Supply comes from the Central belt (85–150 km) and the Rift (160–310 km).
- **Thika, Ruiru, Limuru**: food, beverage, textiles, paper. 45 km from Nairobi, close to the Central belt.
- **Nakuru, Naivasha**: food processing, dairy, horticulture packhouses. Inside the Rift belt (about 160 km to Nairobi).
- **Eldoret**: milling, dairy, textiles. In the Uasin Gishu plantations.
- **Kericho, Nandi, Bomet**: tea factories, at the source.
- **Kisumu and the western sugar belt**: sugar, ethanol, molasses, food.
- **Mombasa**: refineries, edible oils, port-side processing. Long haul (about 485 km), so dense fuels and residues fit best.
