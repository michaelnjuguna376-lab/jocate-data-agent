# Prospect fit score (0-100)

Score every prospect with this rubric and store the breakdown in `score_breakdown`. Use only verified evidence: if a factor isn't evidenced, score it 0 and say so. Don't guess.

| Factor | Points | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| Heat demand (sector tier) | 0-30 | Tier A sector: 30. Tier B: 15. Unclear: 0. Tiers are in `market-brief.md` |
| Haul distance | 0-20 | Site within about 150 road km of a supply belt: 20. 150-300 km: 14. Over 300 km: 6 |
| Fossil steam fuel evidenced | 0-15 | Documented HFO, IDO, diesel or LPG boilers: 15. Sector norm but undocumented: 5 |
| Scale | 0-15 | Multi-site or large plant (national brand, large output, 200+ staff): 15. Medium: 8. Small: 3 |
| Switching signals | 0-15 | 5 each, up to 15: ESG or net-zero target, recent expansion, energy audit, a peer switch in the same town, a biomass or firewood tender |
| Accessibility | 0-5 | Public supplier-registration or tender portal, or a procurement contact page: 5 |

**Bands:** 75+ **Priority**: approach this month. 55-74 **Strong**. 35-54 **Watch**. Under 35: don't list.

**Confidence** (separate from score): `high` if two or more independent sources back the key facts, `medium` if one primary source does, `low` if it rests only on secondary or older (over 24 months) sources. Low-confidence prospects go in the report's "verify before approaching" list.
